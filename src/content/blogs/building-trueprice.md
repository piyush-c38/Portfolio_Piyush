Online discounts are often presented in a way that feels simple at first and confusing by checkout.

You might see a large sale banner, then discover a bank offer, a coupon, and a cashback reward, each with different rules. The final effective price becomes harder to reason about than it should be.

That confusion became the starting point for TruePrice. The idea is to evaluate stacked offers and calculate the real cost a buyer can expect instead of showing only the loudest discount.

The project is still evolving, but the central product principle is clear: remove ambiguity and help users make better purchase decisions with transparent pricing logic.
